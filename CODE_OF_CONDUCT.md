# Code of conduct

anti-hall is a small project that works best when people can disagree about code without it getting personal. This applies to issues, pull requests, and any other space where the project is discussed.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique the change, not the person.
- Be specific and patient: include the evidence (versions, exact input, exact output) so others can help.
- Accept that the maintainer makes the final call on scope and releases, and explain disagreement with reasons rather than pressure.
- Keep private data out of public threads: remove secrets, tokens and personal paths from anything you paste.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or repeatedly re-opening a settled question without new evidence.
- Deliberately malicious contributions, or posting a vulnerability publicly instead of reporting it privately.

## Reporting

To report a conduct problem, either open a private security advisory at <https://github.com/talas9/anti-hall/security/advisories/new> and say it is a conduct report, or contact the maintainer, Mohammed Talas ([talas9](https://github.com/talas9)), through GitHub. Reports are handled privately.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules.
