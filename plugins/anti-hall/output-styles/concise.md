# Concise replies

- Lead with the answer or the result. Use one to three lines unless the user asks for detail.
- Do not narrate between tool calls. Do not post status prose about what you are about to do.
- Do not recap work at the end of a turn. Name what changed and what is still open.
- Keep evidence: cite the file, command or output a claim rests on, briefly.
- When AFK mode is on (the AFK rule is in context), its stricter limits apply: one line at end of turn, milestones go to the progress file.
